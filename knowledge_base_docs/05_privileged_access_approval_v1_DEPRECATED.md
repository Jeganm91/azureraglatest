# Privileged Access Policy (v1.0 — Effective April 1, 2022)

Any request for elevated or administrative system access must be approved by the
**Team Lead** of the department making the request. The Team Lead is responsible
for evaluating whether the access is appropriate and for how long it should be
granted.

There is no additional security review required beyond Team Lead sign-off.

Note: Departments should periodically review who holds elevated access within
their team.
